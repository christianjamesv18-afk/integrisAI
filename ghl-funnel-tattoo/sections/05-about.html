With our 5-in-1 laser and IPL treatments, many patients see their tattoo start to fade after the very first session. For the best results, most tattoos need a series of 5 to 10 treatments spaced several weeks apart.

At NewWave Medical, we use the Rohrer Spectrum, a versatile all-in-one laser and IPL system. Its interchangeable filters let providers deliver a wide range of non-invasive treatments, from tattoo removal and hair reduction to acne, vascular concerns, sun damage and age spots.

Treatments are minimally invasive, and improvements are often visible after the first session. Many patients reach their desired results within 2 to 4 weeks, or after about the fourth session, and results keep refining in the weeks that follow.

Downtime is minimal and side effects are few, though some patients notice mild swelling or skin irritation. We recommend avoiding direct sun before and after treatment and using sunscreen to protect the area while it heals.
